[h1]A history for every province on the map[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16549542523539939086/92623A5CB15405E0B961E4CEF23DF324462C8D45/[/img]

Adds a description to all [b]3,272 land provinces[/b]: what the place is, how it stood in 1444, and the people and events that shaped it up to 1821.

Open a province and hover the [b]scroll button[/b] next to the terrain picture.

[list]
[*]Only adds text, no gameplay changes, works on existing saves
[*]Works with or without [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=788906833]Bigger UI[/url]
[*][b]Not achievement compatible[/b]: the scripted button changes the game checksum
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]French, German and Spanish games show the English text
[*]With Bigger UI enabled, its hotkeys for the province window and colonization panel don't work; the rest of Bigger UI is unaffected
[*]The button is on the Province tab, not the State tab
[/list]

Sources are mainly Wikipedia in English and the local language, and every entry was checked against them. Found a mistake? Leave a comment with the province name and I'll fix it.
