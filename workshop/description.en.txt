[h1]A history for every province on the map[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16549542523539939086/92623A5CB15405E0B961E4CEF23DF324462C8D45/[/img]

Adds descriptions to all [b]3,272 provinces[/b]: what the place is, how it stood in 1444, and the people and events that shaped it up to 1821.

[b]How to use: click a province and hover the scroll button next to the terrain picture[/b]

The entries were written from a large body of sources, mostly Wikipedia in the local language, and corrected over several passes. That took a lot of tokens from today's best AI models, but I hope it was worth it! Any feedback is welcome in the comments. Thanks :)

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only adds flavour text, nothing else changes, works with existing saves
[*]Sorry, not compatible with Ironman or achievements; there seems to be no way around it
[*]Works with Bigger UI and other common UI mods
[/list]
