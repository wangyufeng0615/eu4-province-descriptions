[h1]A history for every province on the map[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/16549542523539939086/92623A5CB15405E0B961E4CEF23DF324462C8D45/[/img]

Vanilla EU4 has no province descriptions at all.

This mod adds a description to all [b]3,272[/b] land provinces: where the province lies, what it looked like at the 1444 start, and what happened there up to 1821.

Open any province and hover the scroll button to the right of the terrain picture. For uncolonized provinces the button sits at the right end of the picture.

[list]
[*]French, German and Spanish games show the English text
[*]Works with and without [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=788906833]Bigger UI[/url]
[*]Only adds text; no gameplay changes
[*]Can be added to an existing save
[/list]

Every description is written from published sources, mainly Wikipedia in English and the local language, and was reviewed and corrected entry by entry. If you find a mistake, please leave a comment with the province name. I'll keep maintaining and updating this mod!

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Not achievement compatible: the mod adds a scripted button, which changes the game checksum.
[*]With Bigger UI enabled, the hotkeys it adds to the province window and the colonization panel do not work; everything else in Bigger UI is unaffected.
[*]The button is on the Province tab of the province window, not the State tab.
[*]Descriptions cover 1444–1821 and do not change with the start date.
[/list]
