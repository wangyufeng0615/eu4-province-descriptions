# Sources and credits

`sources/sources.jsonl` (in the mod) and the `sources` field of `data/entries.jsonl` (in this repository) list, for each province, the pages consulted when writing its description. Most are Wikipedia articles in English or the local language; Wikipedia contributors are credited through those pages and their history. The list records research provenance; it does not mean Wikipedia or any other source endorses this mod or that every statement was independently verified.

The descriptions condense, reorganise and translate consulted material with AI assistance, and the source pages are not reproduced. New description text is offered under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (see `LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`): keep these credits and the source links, mark your changes and keep the share-alike terms. Wikimedia's [Terms of Use, section 7](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use#7._Licensing_of_Content) describe reuse of Wikipedia text.

`interface/00_eu4lg_provinceview.gui` is the vanilla EU4 province window definition with one button added; it is Paradox's file, shipped so the button can be shown, and is not relicensed. Chinese text is stored in the encoding of the Chinese Language Mod (Workshop 2976470733) so that it displays when that mod is enabled; no files from that mod are included.
